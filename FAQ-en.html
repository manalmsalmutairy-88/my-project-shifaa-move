What services does the website provide?

We provide educational content about physical therapy and help patients find the right centers for their condition near them.

Are the educational videos free?

Yes, you only need to register on the site to benefit from all available content.

Do you offer physical therapy sessions?

No, we do not provide direct sessions; we only provide guidance to the appropriate center and show some helpful videos and tools.

Can I benefit from applying exercises from the videos?

Yes, for simple cases it is useful, but in advanced cases, it is preferable to consult a specialist.

Are the videos suitable for all cases?

The videos are general and helpful alongside physical therapy, and do not replace physical therapy in specialized centers.
